THE VAULT

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 1, 2026

The short version

THE VAULT has no account system, no login, no name or email field anywhere in the app. It does not know who you are. The only identifier it creates is an anonymous, random ID used solely to keep your leaderboard score attached to a player instead of no player.

What we collect, and why

1. An anonymous player ID

The first time the app needs to submit a leaderboard score, it asks Firebase Authentication to create an anonymous sign-in — a random identifier with no name, email, phone number, or device information attached to it. This ID exists only to tell your leaderboard entries apart from everyone else's. Uninstalling the app, or asking us to delete your data (see below), ends its association with you.

2. Your leaderboard score and, if you choose one, a username

When you earn points, the app stores a running score (an integer) and a server timestamp against your anonymous ID in our Firestore database. That's the complete leaderboard record, unless you also choose a username.

The first time you reach the home screen, you can optionally pick a name for the leaderboard (3–20 letters, numbers, or underscores). This is the one piece of information in this app that you actively type in yourself, and the one piece that's shown publicly: every other player sees it next to your score. Choosing one is entirely optional — you can skip it and stay anonymous, in which case your row just shows a generic "Player" label like everyone else who hasn't chosen a name. Please don't use your real name or anything else identifying as your username — we don't verify or restrict what it says beyond its length and character set, so treat it the same way you'd treat any public handle.

3. Advertising identifiers (non-Premium players only)

THE VAULT shows ads through Google's AdMob network unless you've purchased the one-time Premium upgrade. To do this, Google's Mobile Ads SDK processes your device's advertising identifier and related technical information (approximate location derived from IP, device type, etc.) to select and measure ads. This is Google acting as an independent advertiser, not as our service provider — see "Third parties" below. You can reset or limit this identifier at any time in your device's system settings.

4. Purchase status (Premium players only)

If you buy the Premium upgrade, Apple's App Store or Google Play processes the transaction directly — we never see your payment card, billing address, or other payment details. Our app only receives a "purchased" confirmation from the store, which it stores on your device to remember your entitlement.

5. Everything else — stored only on your device

Your campaign progress, stars, best times, hint/undo counts, settings, and narrative-unlock state are all stored locally on your device and are never transmitted anywhere. Reinstalling the app or clearing its storage erases this permanently — we have no copy of it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your real name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, precise location, health data, or any government identifier. THE VAULT has no chat, comments, or any way for one player to send content to another — the only things this app ever sends anywhere are the score and, if you choose to set one, the username described above.

Third parties

Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore) — our backend infrastructure provider for the leaderboard. Firebase processes data on our behalf under Google's standard data-processing terms; it does not use this data for its own purposes.

Google AdMob — an independent advertising network. AdMob's own privacy policy governs how it uses advertising identifiers: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Apple App Store / Google Play Billing — process in-app purchases directly; we never receive your payment details.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone beyond the service providers listed above.

Children

THE VAULT is not directed at children. Its narrative deals with themes of judgment, guilt, imprisonment, and suffering, and it is not designed or marketed for a young audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and deletion

Your leaderboard entry is retained until you ask us to remove it, or until the leaderboard service is discontinued. Because the app has no login, we cannot look up "your" entry by email or account — to request deletion, contact us below. If you chose a username, just tell us what it is (usernames are unique, so this is an exact match); if you stayed anonymous, give us your approximate score and the date you last played and we'll locate and remove the matching record on a best-effort basis. Locally stored progress can be erased at any time by uninstalling the app.

Security

Data in transit between the app and our backend (Firebase Authentication, Firestore) is encrypted using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes will be reflected in the app's Play Store listing.
